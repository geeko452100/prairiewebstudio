Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our web design process, pricing, and local SEO for Great Bend businesses.

How long does it take to launch a new website? +

Most Prairie Web Studio projects launch within 7 business days after we receive your content and brand details. Larger multi-page sites may take up to two weeks.

Do you only work with businesses in Great Bend? +

We specialize in Great Bend and Central Kansas, but we work remotely with small businesses across Kansas. Every site includes local SEO setup tailored to your service area.

What SEO is included with every website? +

Every site comes ready to be found. We write clear page titles and descriptions, add your business name, hours, and location in the format Google reads, and include a sitemap so search engines can list all your pages. The technical SEO is handled behind the scenes — you don't have to think about any of it.

Will my website score 90-100 on Google Lighthouse? +

Yes — we build every site to score 90-100 on Google's Lighthouse checks for speed, ease of use, and search-friendliness. In plain terms: your pages load fast, work great on phones, and look good to Google. No clunky page builders weighing things down.

Do I own my website after launch? +

With Own It Outright, you own the site from day one. Launch & Support lowers your upfront build cost. Either build plan can pair with a maintenance subscription for ongoing updates and monitoring — we can discuss ownership transfer anytime.
