Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our web design process, pricing, and local SEO for Great Bend businesses.

How long does it take to launch a new website? +

Most Snap Load Digital projects launch within 7 business days after we receive your content and brand details. Larger multi-page sites may take up to two weeks.

Do you only work with businesses in Great Bend? +

We specialize in Great Bend and Central Kansas, but we work remotely with small businesses across Kansas. Every site includes local SEO setup tailored to your service area.

What SEO is included with every website? +

Every build includes optimized title tags and meta descriptions, canonical URLs, structured data (JSON-LD), a sitemap, robots.txt, semantic HTML, and local business schema so Google can understand your location and services.

Will my website score 100 on Google Lighthouse? +

Yes — we engineer every site for 100 Lighthouse scores in Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, and SEO using static HTML, purged CSS, and no bloated page builders.

Do I own my website after launch? +

With the Own It Outright plan, you own the site from day one. With Launch & Support, you get full use of the site while subscribed; we can discuss ownership transfer anytime.
